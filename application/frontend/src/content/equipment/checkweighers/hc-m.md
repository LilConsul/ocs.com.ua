en-body: |
  ## Flexible Checkweighers for Medium Performance Ranges

  The dynamic checkweighers of the HC-M series provide the perfect weighing technology for challenging medium range checkweighing applications. They are the link between entry-level and high-performance scales, delivering up to 250 weight values per minute for the complete medium range.

  ## Key Features

  * **Complete Stainless Steel Construction** - Entire machine housing with integrated infeed, weighing, and outfeed belts
  * **EMFR Weigh Cell Technology** - Electro Magnetic Force Restoration for superior accuracy
  * **Patented Conveyor System** - Lightweight design ensures smooth, fast and safe product transport
  * **Versatile Sorting Options** - Wide range of rejection mechanisms (pusher, air blast) for reliable ejection
  * **Compact Footprint** - Optional double deck design allows complete weight check on two lanes

  ## Your Benefits

  * **Maximum Output** - Up to 250 products per minute throughput
  * **Exact Fill Results** - Higher productivity through precise measurements
  * **100% Product Inspection** - Compliant with national prepackage regulations
  * **Reliable Sorting** - Incorrect weight products ejected without production interference
  * **Sophisticated Drive Concept** - Carefully tuned for optimal performance

  ## Reliable, Precise, Versatile

  The HC-M is the most convenient answer for multiple weighing tasks: current weight verification, completeness checks, sorting operations, or classification processes. The solid stainless steel base frame enables high precision weight determination at average to high conveyor speeds.

  ## Technical Specifications

  ### Weighing Performance

  * Weighing range: 0-3000g
  * Accuracy: ±0.1g
  * Belt speed: up to 60 m/min
  * Throughput: 250 products/minute
  * Weight values per minute: 250

  ### Physical Dimensions

  * Belt width: 300mm
  * Belt length: 600mm
  * Overall dimensions: 1200 x 800 x 1400mm
  * Weight: 120kg
  * Construction: Stainless steel

  ## Applications

  Perfect for pharmaceutical, food processing, cosmetics, and chemical industries where precise weight control is critical for regulatory compliance and quality assurance. Suitable for completeness checks, sorting, classification, and weight verification tasks.

  ## Modularity for Mid-Range Applications

  The HC-M checkweigher is available in double deck design, allowing complete weight checks on two independent lanes with the space requirement of only one machine. Both systems weigh and process data independently, perfectly adapted to customer needs.
