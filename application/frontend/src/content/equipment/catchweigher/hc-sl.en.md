## High Weighing Accuracy and Parcel Throughput

HC-SL series scales are designed for products weighing up to 60 kg. The high weighing accuracy (up to 5 g) allows speeds of up to 2.9 m/s in calibrated mode. These excellent figures are achieved thanks to a sandwich construction that creates a rigid platform for the parcel travelling across the weighing bridge. It also keeps the noise level below 65 dB.

## Key Features

* Sandwich construction for maximum rigidity
* Weighing range up to 60 kg (optionally 150 kg)
* Calibration value above ±5 g
* Low noise level not exceeding 65 dB
* Communication protocols: Ethernet/IP, Profinet, Profibus
* Symmetrical belt construction for bidirectional use

## Your Benefits

* EMFR weigh cells for the best weighing results
* Double the speed compared to standard machines
* Maintenance-free and wear-free operation
* Weighing during acceleration/deceleration and on slopes
* Flexible motor mounting (left or right)

## Robust Design for Demanding Environments

SL series scales are particularly easy to integrate. Their EMFR weigh cells are completely maintenance-free and wear-free, ensuring long-term reliability in high-throughput logistics environments.

The catchweighers can weigh accurately during acceleration and deceleration phases, as well as on inclines or declines. The scales can be used from both sides thanks to the symmetrical belt construction. Finger protection at the outfeed is fitted as standard for operator safety.

The drive motor can optionally be mounted on the left or right in the direction of belt travel. An internal belt drive (optional) can be used where a particularly narrow design matters, maximising space efficiency in tight warehouse layouts.

## Advanced Weighing Technology

Active Vibration Compensation (AVC) technologies ensure stable performance even in environments with significant vibration from nearby sorting equipment or heavy traffic.

The available weighing belt lengths and widths allow flexible integration into existing or planned transport systems, making the HC-SL an ideal solution for modern distribution centres and logistics operations.
