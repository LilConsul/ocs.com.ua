## High Weighing Accuracy and Throughput of Parcels

Scales of the HC-SL series are designed for products up to a maximum 60 kg. The high level of weighing accuracy (up to 5 g) permits speeds of up to 2.9 m/s in calibrated mode. These excellent values are achieved by the sandwich construction which creates a rigid platform for the parcel to travel over the scale's weigh bridge. It also ensures a noise reduction to below 65 dB.

## Key Features

* Sandwich construction for maximum rigidity
* Weighing range up to 60 kg (optionally 150 kg)
* Calibration value above ±5g
* Low noise level not exceeding 65 dB
* Communication protocols: Ethernet/IP, Profinet, Profibus
* Symmetrical belt construction for bidirectional use

## Your Benefits

* EMFR weigh cells for best weighing results
* Double the speed compared to standard machines
* Maintenance-free and wear-free operation
* Weighing during acceleration/deceleration and on slopes
* Flexible motor mounting (left or right)

## Robust Design for Demanding Environments

Scales of the SL series are particularly easy to integrate. Their EMFR weigh cells are absolutely maintenance- and wear-free, ensuring long-term reliability in high-throughput logistics environments.

The catchweighers can weigh accurately during acceleration and deceleration phases, and on ascending or descending slopes. The scales can be used from both sides due to the symmetrical belt construction. Exit side finger protection is fitted as standard for operator safety.

The drive motor can be mounted optionally on the left or right in the belt's direction of travel. An internal belt drive (optional) can be used where a particularly narrow design is important, maximizing space efficiency in tight warehouse layouts.

## Advanced Weighing Technology

Active Vibration Compensation (AVC) and Weighing Accuracy Analytics technologies ensure consistent performance even in environments with significant vibrations from nearby sorting equipment or heavy traffic.

Available weighing belt lengths and widths allow flexible integration into existing or planned transport systems, making the HC-SL an ideal solution for modern distribution centers and logistics operations.
