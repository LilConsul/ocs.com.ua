## Serialisation with Tamper Evidence

Thanks to tamper-evident labelling systems for the flaps on both sides, the Wipotec TQS-HC-A-TE can serialise individual packs, verify the print, apply tamper-evident labels, determine the pack weight and reject packs according to different settings.

## Five Functions in One Machine

Serialisation, tamper-evident labels, a labelling machine for applying vignettes, additional label printing and dynamic checkweighing in one machine make the TQS-HC-A-TE the most compact serialisation machine in the world. It saves space on the line and offers an excellent price-performance ratio.

## Two Reject Zones

Products with incorrect coding are automatically rejected into a separate bin. Products with incorrect weight are rejected into another bin.

## Single User Interface

All system elements can be operated intuitively and conveniently from a single central user interface, without switching between programs.

## Main Components

* thermal inkjet printer for marking the pack on one side of the flap;
* 1 camera for reading and verifying the information on the flaps;
* applicators for tamper-evident labels on 2 flaps of the folding carton;
* sensors checking for the tamper-evident label on the flaps;
* checkweigher;
* reject device (2 pcs) for rejecting packs with detected flap marking errors, tamper-evident label errors or incorrect weight.
