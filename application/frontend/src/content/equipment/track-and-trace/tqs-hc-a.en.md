## Serialisation with Weight Check

The Wipotec TQS-HC-A combines serialisation of folding cartons with a final completeness check using a checkweigher. The cartons arrive from the cartoner onto an infeed conveyor with an adjustable upper hold-down conveyor. This high-precision pack feed is the basis for accurate marking and final verification.

A detailed description of the checkweigher is available on the [HC-A](/en/catalogue/equipment/checkweigher/hc-a/) model page.

## Two Reject Zones

Products with incorrect codes are rejected into a separate bin. Packs with incorrect weight are rejected into a second bin, after the scale.

## Single User Interface

Print data is sent directly to the printer and the camera. All system elements can be operated quickly and easily from a single user interface, without switching between programs.

## Main Components

* thermal inkjet printer for marking the pack on one side of the flap;
* 1 camera for reading and verifying the information on the flaps;
* checkweigher;
* reject device (2 pcs) for rejecting packs with detected flap marking errors and incorrect weight.
