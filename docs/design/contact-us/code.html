Frequently Asked Questions

Engineering Consultation & OEM Standards

Clear answers regarding compliance verification, trial product testing, spare parts lead times, and factory warranties.

How quickly can certified engineers arrive on-site for a factory line audit?

expand_more

For industrial facilities in the Kyiv region, an application engineer can arrive on-site within 4 hours. For regional facilities across Western (Lviv, Rivne, Ivano-Frankivsk), Southern (Odesa, Mykolaiv), and Central/Eastern Ukraine (Dnipro, Poltava), planned engineering visits are scheduled within 24 to 48 hours depending on plant availability.

How do you handle factory warranty and OEM spare parts from Germany?

expand_more

As the direct authorized distributor for WIPOTEC GmbH (Kaiserslautern) and MESUTRONIC Gerätebau GmbH (Kirchberg im Wald), OS-Technology Ukraine delivers 100% factory-original equipment, components, and EMFR weigh cells under official European warranty. We maintain a large strategic safety stock of belts, bearings, sensors, and electronic boards in our Kyiv warehouse for immediate domestic dispatch.

Can we test sample products on Wipotec or Mesutronic machines before ordering?

expand_more

Yes. We strongly encourage pre-purchase product characterization. You can ship sample test batches (packaged goods, blisters, bags, foil packs) to our Kyiv testing facility. Our engineers will perform comprehensive dynamic speed tests, determine optimal rejector mechanics, and generate an official measurement report confirming achievable weighing discreteness and detection sensitivity (Fe, Non-Fe, SS316, ceramic, bone, or glass).

What documentation is provided for pharmaceutical OIML and GMP compliance?

expand_more

We provide full qualification packages including Design Qualification (DQ), Installation Qualification (IQ), and Operational Qualification (OQ) protocols aligned with EU GMP Annex 11 and Annex 15. All dynamic scales are supplied with Ukrainian Module B & F verification documentation according to Technical Regulations for non-automatic and automatic weighing instruments (OIML R51). Software complies with FDA 21 CFR Part 11 audit trails.
